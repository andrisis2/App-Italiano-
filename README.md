# Italiano B2–C1

Advanced Italian for English speakers: a static PWA (one `index.html` + `knowledge.json`),
live at **https://andrisis2.github.io/App-Italiano-/**.

- **Daily review**: ten mixed activities a day (words, a verb, sentences, grammar, articles, false friends, the atlas), with spaced repetition.
- **Grand Tour d'Italia**: twelve stages from Turin to Palermo, each with its own grammar theme, reading, dialogue and place.
- **Read & listen**: city readings and C1 essays; tap any word for its translation and grammar, or open *Explain* for a word-by-word analysis.
- **Dialogues and phrasebook**: B2/C1 conversations to listen to and act out with the microphone; 127 phrases by register.
- **Grammar gym**: 14 topics (subjunctive, hypothetical sentences, combined pronouns, passato remoto…) with theory and 224 questions.
- **Italy atlas**: 55 monuments, landscapes and dishes, with texts, key words and quizzes.
- **Words and verbs**: about 2,000 flashcard words (B2 and C1) and 260 fully conjugated verbs, plus automatic conjugation of regular ones.
- **Andrea**, an AI voice tutor (bring your own Gemini key): free conversation, corrections, role-play and debate.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the architecture.
